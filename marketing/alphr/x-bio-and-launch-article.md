# The market has millions of opinions. Someone should keep the score.

**Alphr is live: a daily crypto forecasting game where you, the crowd and three AI analysts face the same question under the same rules.**

---

Everyone on crypto Twitter has a view on where the market goes next.

Almost nobody has a record.

"Called it" gets posted after the move. The misses quietly disappear. Confidence is loud, and accountability is optional. After a while it becomes impossible to tell who actually reads the market well and who is simply the most visible.

We wanted a place where that question has an answer. So we built **Alphr**.

## One question a day

Every day there is one Round: one asset (BTC, ETH or SOL), one 24-hour window, one question.

**Where does it close: Bullish, Neutral or Bearish?**

To make your call you:

1. **Pick a direction.** Bullish, Neutral or Bearish. A small neutral band around zero means "flat" is a real answer, not a coin flip.
2. **Cite exactly three signals.** Momentum, Volume, Market Trend, Fear & Greed and more. Three, no more and no less, so every call has a stated reason.
3. **Set your confidence** from 1 to 5, and optionally add a short thesis.
4. **Lock it.** Once locked, the call is timestamped and cannot be edited or deleted.

When the Round settles, the result is calculated from the recorded start and end prices. Right or wrong, it goes on your record.

## You can't see the crowd until you commit

This is the rule we care about most.

How the crowd is leaning stays hidden until you have locked your own call. The same goes for the AI analysts' positions.

No anchoring. No following the herd. You commit first, then you find out whether you were alone.

## Meet the machines

Three AI analysts play every Round, under exactly the same rules as everyone else:

- **ATLAS** reads trend, momentum and volume together.
- **PULSE** follows pure momentum.
- **DRIFT** reads sentiment and fades moves that look crowded.

They are rule-based simulations, not chatbots, and their methodology is public. They lock before the deadline and cannot see the answer. Their calls are never counted in the crowd.

So every day there are three questions: did you beat the market, did you beat the crowd, and did you beat the machines?

## Every call becomes a receipt

Each locked call becomes a public **Call Card**: the asset, your direction, your three signals, your confidence and, after settlement, the result printed on it.

Share it before the Round settles. That is the point. "I called it" is free. Locked and timestamped is not.

## A track record that means something

Over time, Alphr turns your calls into a picture of how you think:

- **Leaderboard:** weekly, monthly and all-time, per asset. You are ranked once you have five settled Rounds, so a single lucky call cannot top the board.
- **Call Profile:** which signals you lean on, which ones actually work for you, and whether your confidence matches reality.
- **Humans vs AI:** a running scoreboard of how people as a whole are doing against the three machines.

The full scoring formula is published on the methodology page. Nothing is hidden.

## What Alphr is not

Alphr runs on **virtual points only**.

No wallet. No deposits. No trades. No stakes. No prizes. Nothing you do on Alphr touches real money, and nothing on Alphr is financial advice. It is a game about reading the market and being honest about how well you do it.
